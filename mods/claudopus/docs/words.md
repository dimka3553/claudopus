# How Claudopus says things

Every word Claudopus shows you (panel, cards, the bar, questions, notices, results, Config, Stats, Memory, About) follows this page.

## Who does what
- Four can act: **you**, **the chat** (where you type), **Claude** (doing a task in the background) and **Claudopus**. A sentence in a notice or a page names who does what: "Claudopus told Claude", never "the work was told". A card's line, a row and a to-do name neither Claude nor Claudopus: they say what is being done.
- One word for each thing, the same everywhere:

| Say | For | Never |
|---|---|---|
| task | a job the chat started, shown as a card | work, job, piece of work |
| helper | a Claude that a task starts | agent, subagent |
| command | anything run on the computer: a script, download, server, watch | run, process, shell, job |
| question | what Claude asks you | ask (as a noun) |
| to-do | a line on a task's list | step |
| result | what a finished task leaves you | report, finding |
| topic | a line in Memory with its notes | subject, stream |
| full | how much of its memory a chat or task has used | context, tokens, k (Stats alone counts tokens, and says what they are) |

- Never: brief, steer, turn, hand back, printed, killed, exit code, flag, shipped, mod, legwork.

## Sentences
- Everyday words, the fewest that are exact. Present tense, active voice, contractions (don't, isn't, can't).
- Sentence case everywhere. A card line is a fragment with no full stop; a sentence ends with one.
- Join parts of a status line with " · ". No semicolons, brackets or "~" in a status line: say "about".
- One idea per line. Say what happens next or what you can do, never why the code did it.

## Standard forms
- **States**, one word each, one colour each, and no others: Working (purple), Waiting for you (amber), Delivered (blue), Done (green), Stopped (grey), Failed (red), Interrupted (orange). Working is from a task's start until its result, whether it takes steps or a command or helper of its own runs. Waiting for you is only while a question, a permission, or a key or password box of its own waits for you; amber means nothing else. A task that hands in its result still asking you something (such as whether to release) has that as its question, the same block as any other, with the buttons it gave (`Release it`, `Don't release`; `Yes` and `No` for a yes-or-no question it gave none for) and `Answer in words`; a press, your words, or a message you send the task is its answer, and starts it again, purple. Delivered means one thing only: the task handed in its result, it asks you nothing more, every to-do is ticked or dropped, and no command or helper of its own still runs (a server it left for you keeps its row under the card, with Stop). Stopped is only your Stop, or the chat's on your word. A Delivered card is yours to look at: its line is `◐ Delivered · <the task's last note>`, such as `◐ Delivered · Released as 0.2.206`, and its only two presses are at its bottom, `Give feedback`, which opens the box above the chat and puts the task back to work on the same card, and `Mark as done`. Done is only your Mark as done: the card turns green where it stands, outlined, with the happy octopus and the chime, says `✓ Done · <the task's last note>`, keeps its shape, its two presses gone at once, and two seconds after it first shows green goes under Finished, where it keeps Message. The cards stand in this order: what waits for you first, then what was delivered, the newest on top, then what works and what stopped or failed, each in the place it began in. A Stopped or Failed card stays where it is until you press Dismiss, which puts it under Finished; nothing else moves a card that has ended. Failed says why: `Failed · Ended without its result`, with Start again. Interrupted is only a task the app's close (a quit, a restart, a crash, a rewind of the chat) cut off before its result: `⏸ Interrupted · The app closed while it worked`, or one that no message can reach any more, such as a task a rewind left behind: `⏸ Interrupted · Can't be reached any more`, which goes under Finished when it had already handed in its result, a message you wrote to it marked `Didn't reach the task · the chat has it`. Each has Carry on, which has a fresh task take it up from what Claudopus kept of it, never Done. A question it asked still waits for you, and your answer carries it on. A task of another chat the app's close cut off shows under that chat's name until that chat is opened.
- A card's top row is its facts alone: model, how full, to-dos and time. Its stage is its colour, and once it has ended the line under the top row begins with it: `◐ Delivered`, `✓ Done`, `Stopped`, `✕ Failed · <why>`, `⏸ Interrupted · The app closed while it worked`. Details begins its top row with the stage.
- The panel's header counts by stage: `1 waiting for you · 2 working`, `2 working · 1 delivered`, `1 delivered`, `1 working · 2 interrupted`, `Just finished`, `Nothing waits for you`.
- Under the header, one row of tabs on the panel's ground, the one picked bright and underlined, each with how many it holds once there are any: `All`, `For you 2` (what waits on you: a question or a result to look at), `Working 3`, `Finished`, `Starred 1`. Finished lists every task that ended, its result a press away, and Starred every task with ★, even once finished, dismissed or its chat gone. A long list shows a page of 40 at a time, with `Newer`, `41–80 of 1,962` and `Older` under it; `Search` at its heading's right opens `Search tasks by name`, and `Close` ends the search. `☆` at a card's top right stars it.
- **Time running** `29m 40s`, over an hour `1h 05m`. Never a guessed time: no "of about", no "over". **Ago** `2m ago`. **Left**, only when the command itself prints one: `1m left`. **Dates** `11 Nov, 14:20`.
- **Counts** `<n> of <total> <what>`: `2 of 6 stills`, `2 of 5 to-dos`.
- **Buttons** are a verb of one to three words, the same word for the same act: Allow, Don't allow, Type it, Paste it, Don't give it, Answer in words, Release it (or what the go-ahead does in the project: Push it, Merge it, Open the pull request, Put it live), Send, Stop and send, Attach, Paste, Close, Stop, Message, Reply, Give feedback, Mark as done, Carry on, Dismiss, Details, Result, Refresh, Open chat, Open in Terminal, Try again, Free up space, Pick up again, Search, Close.
- **Headings** are a noun: To-dos, Helpers and commands, Finished, Result.

## Every running thing has a progress line
A running command or helper is two lines:
1. **What it does**, with how long it has run: its kind, then a plain title a small model writes from the command, the task's words and its log, `Test · Score smoothed heads against the 47 test films · 4m 10s`, or the title alone where it starts with its kind, `Render the song stills`. Where the model gives none, the task's own words.
2. **Now**: how far in things · how fast · what it is doing now: `2 of 6 · 33% · rendering still 3`. It counts things, never time; a time left shows only when the command itself prints one.

- After three minutes with nothing new, line 2 keeps the last thing it said and adds how long: `Last: 2 of 6 stills rendered · nothing new for 29m`. With nothing ever said: `No output yet · started 29m ago`. Never "nothing new" alone. A narrow row keeps the time: `No output yet · 29m`, `No progress for 29m`.
- A cloud job is never called busy or idle, as its work is on another computer. Silent, it says `Nothing back from the cloud yet · started 48m ago · still waiting on the cloud machine`, in amber, and its task is told to bring the job's own output back, which then shows as any command's does, in its row and its log.
- Every row has Stop. Only commands the app itself tracks have rows: nothing runs out of its sight.
- A server: `Serving`, and its row stays under a Done card until you stop it. A watch for something far away: `Watching · last: series arrived`.
- A helper's line 2 is what it is doing now, in plain words: `Reading files`, `Rendering the song stills · 4m`, `Searching the web`, `Waiting for its commands`, or its own last note. Quiet for three minutes, it is `Last: Reading files · nothing new for 29m`.
- A task's card says only its stage, the to-do marked doing (`To-do 5 of 7 · Mend the nose`), and its own last note as its line, plus in amber what needs you. A paused task is Working, and its card says nothing of the pause; a command that ended never takes the place of the task's own line, nor does a command you allowed: its end is a grey line where it ran for a minute, `Your command ended after 20s`, and a row of the card's ended commands. Before the task's first note, its line is what it is doing now, `Reading files`, or `Thinking` just after a step.
- Ended: `Done in 2m 05s`, `Failed after 2m 05s`, `Stopped after 1m`, `Ran out of time after 30m`.
- Line 2's plain words are the command's own `progress:` words, else what the model says it does now, `Waiting for the cloud machine to start`. A failed command says why, in the model's plain words, under its row in Details and in its log: `Ran out of memory while loading the films`.
- A count that is full while the command still runs never says 100%, and nothing after it: `7 of 7 heads · still running`.
- In Details the commands of a task or helper that ended are a group, last under it, headed by a row as its card's closing row: `3 commands ended · 1 failed`. A press on that row folds them away (`3 commands ended · show`). Each is a row under it: its name as it had it running, never its shell line, then how long it ran, `2m 41s`, or how it ended where it did not finish, `Failed after 20s`. A press opens its log, which says when it ended.
- A card's closing row counts what ended and what did not finish, and names none: `13 commands ended · 1 failed, 2 ran out of time`, `2 helpers ended · 1 helper failed`. A failed command has no row of its own on the card: which one, how and why is in Details and its log.
- A press on a command's name opens its log in a tab of the panel, running or ended: its name, how it stands, `Stop`, `Details`, `Open in Terminal`, then the end of what it wrote. Details repeats none of it under a command.

- A task's conversation in Details reads as the app's own chat, oldest first, with no marks, and while the task works its last lines are what it does right now, moving as it happens: `Thinking…`, then `▸ Thought for 12s`, which opens on its thinking where the app gives it, its words as they are written, each command as it is written and while it runs in the app's chat's words over its own name, `Running a command · 40s` over `Fit the left nostril`, which joins the folded `Ran 3 commands` once it ends, then each helper at work, `Helper: <its title>` over what it does now, a press opening it.

## Files a task changed
- A card says `3 changed files` beside its other files, a press that opens Details. Under Files there, the files a task changed in a repo are a table by kind, `Code 5 files`, then `Documents 2 files`, each heading with its counts, the most changed first, under a dim line for each folder in the repo: a row a file with its icon (its ending in its language's colour), its name, `new` for a file it made, then `+259 −24` and a bar (green for lines added, red for lines removed, as long as its change against the most changed) in columns that line up down the list. Past eight, `Show all 12`.
- A press on a file's name shows it in the app's Files pane beside the chat. Where the app can't (the terminal, or a file outside the chat's folders), what changed in it opens in a tab of the panel, named after the file: its name, its folder and counts, `Details`, `Whole file` or `Changes`, then the changes as Claude Code draws a diff.

## Marks
One mark for each kind of row, the same on a card and in Details; a ring moves around one that runs.

| Mark | Kind | Its row begins | With nothing of its own to say |
|---|---|---|---|
| ● purple | helper | its title | `Thinking`, or what its step does: `Reading files`, `Searching the web` |
| ■ teal | command | `Run …`, or its own verb: `Build …`, `Test …`, `Render …` | `Running`, `Building`, `Testing` |
| □ teal | command on another computer (ssh, a cloud job, a cluster) | the same | `Running on another computer` |
| ▼ blue | download | `Download …` | `Downloading` |
| ▲ blue | upload | `Upload …` | `Uploading` |
| ◆ green | server | `Serve …` | `Serving` |
| ◇ grey | watch for something far away, such as a cloud job | `Watch …` | `Watching` |
| ■ grey | the commands folded away | `3 more commands` | |
| the same shape in red | a command that failed, in Details | its name | `Failed after 2m 05s` |
| ○ grey | what has ended | `13 commands ended` | |

## Results in the chat
- The chat says nothing of a task's progress: that is on its card. It posts a task's result, and one line when the task needs you.
- A result reaches the chat as its **short result**: at most five short lines saying what changed, whether it is live, and what you have to do or that nothing is needed. No table, heading, commit, branch or list of files. The rest stays in the saved result, which `Result` on the reply opens.
- A reply about a task sits in a box headed by the task's colour dot and name, then `Result` where it passes one on, then `Reply`. The box's edge is a card's colour, never the task's: blue for a result, purple with `Part · still working` for a part handed in by a task still at work, grey for anything else. Once you mark the task done, its results and parts turn green, as its card does. Two results in one reply stand in a box each.
- A go-ahead is named for what it does in the project, never `release` where the project has no releases: `Push the sign-in fix to main?` with `Push it`.
- A question or go-ahead with buttons also shows in the chat, once, when its task stops for you or hands in its result: the task's dot and name, then the same block as on its card, and a press there answers it. Once answered it is one grey line, `✓ Answered`. A password, a key and disk space stay in the panel.

## Questions, notices, errors
- **Everything that waits for you is one block**, the same on the card, in Details, at the top of the panel and in the chat: a small label (Permission, Question, Password, Key, Disk space), the question in amber ending in "?", then what it is about in short lines, the command whole, a quiet row of the values in it you would paste elsewhere (an account or case number, an id, an email, a link, a path, a key's name), each `⧉ <value>` and `✓ Copied` for two seconds once pressed, and the buttons. A result has the same row under it, in Details and in its own tab.
- **Permission**: `Allow`, `Don't allow`; an admin command also has `Always allow this command`; a task's own also has `Answer in words`, whose words go to the task, which answers inside the block, and allow nothing. Once you answer in words, the card folds the block to one grey line of your words, as an answered question, with nothing to press, until the task's answer brings it back with its buttons. `Run this as admin on this Mac?` `Push <branch> to main?`, or `Release <branch> to main?` where the project numbers its versions. `Claude Code blocked this command. Run it anyway?` A release Claude Code blocked after your press allowed it says so, and why it needs a second press: `Claude Code blocked the release you allowed. Run it anyway?` A connector's action Claude Code blocked is a question with the command that allows it in your own settings: `Claude Code blocked this action. Let your tasks do it?`, `Open in Terminal`, `Try again`, `Don't allow`. A change Claude Code blocked in a folder outside the chat's asks `Claude Code blocked changes in <folder>. Let this chat work there?` with `/add-dir <folder>` to type in the chat, `Try again` and `Don't allow`, and a command blocked there says that folder is why. Typing `allow`, a yes or a button's own words in the chat is your press on the newest block it fits, or on the one whose task it names; while a release waits, any other message you type is read for what it means, in whatever words: one that plainly tells to go ahead is its press (`push it so I can test`, `yes, go`), for every release that waits where it says so, and a question, a hold or talk of something else (`is it pushed?`, `don't push yet`, `the push notifications look good`) presses nothing; the line above the prompt says `● <its question> · <task>` with its own yes button, as on its card, for as long as it waits; a command Claude Code blocked says `Only your press on Allow runs it. Typing allow in the chat doesn't.` A release says what it puts on main: the task's own line for it, else `Claude gave no reason for it. Its commits say:`, its commits, then `3 commits · 12 changed files · branch <branch> · from <folder>`.
- **Disk space**, at the top of the panel while the disk has less than 20 GB left: `Your Mac has 12 GB of disk space left. Free up space?`, what waits below 5 GB and what the press removes, then `Free up space`. While it runs: `Freeing up space`; then `Freed 9 GB`.
- **Password**: `Type it`, `Don't allow`. **Key**: `Paste it`, `Don't give it`, `Answer in words`. A task that asks you for a key shows its key's block at once with its own question, then what it is about, `Where to get it:` with the link and what to press, `It replaces the SENTRY_AUTH_TOKEN saved now.` or `SENTRY_AUTH_TOKEN stays saved as it is: this is a second key.`, and `It goes only into this Mac's keychain.` No other question asks for a key, so no question has a button such as `Key is in`.
- **Question**: the task's own buttons, then `Answer in words`, quiet. Every button is an answer that carries the task on: there is no `Not now`, as not pressing leaves it open, and no `Skip` or `Set aside`: `☆` on the card keeps a task, and `Dismiss` or `Mark as done` lets it go.
- **Message** is a quiet button at the top right of a card that works or has finished: it opens the same box, and what you send goes straight to that task, which carries on with it. A delivered card has `Reply` and `Mark as done` at its bottom instead, and nothing at its top right; a stopped or failed card has `Dismiss` (puts it under Finished and sends nothing). The same box, the same message, sits at the bottom of the task's Details. Under it: `Attach` and `Paste picture`, then, while the task works, `Stop and send` (stops what it is doing, with its commands and helpers, and starts it again with the message), and `Send` (it reads it at its next step). Above the box in Details, your messages to that task are listed in the order you sent them, numbered, each with its time and `Not read yet` or `✓ Read 14:20`, plus `the same words as 1` for a repeat and `you stopped its step to send it`. While one waits, a line says what it waits behind: `The task reads messages between its steps. Its current step, “Release the photo move”, has run 1h 58m.` The card's grey line says `✓ You wrote “…” · not read yet` until the task reads it, then `· read`. In the conversation your words are headed `You · sent 14:20 · read 14:21`. Stop and send gives the task every message it hasn't read yet, in the order you sent them. A file picked shows as `📎 <name> ✕`, a picture with its small copy above; a press takes it off. The box that answers a question in words and Message on a result have `Attach` and `Paste picture` too.
- **Typing** happens in one box above the chat: a heading that says what it is for, the field, `Send` and `Close`; Enter sends. No line under the field explains it. Filled buttons go to the task; the quiet `Close` and `Dismiss` never do.
- **After a press** the block goes at once and one grey line says what you did: `✓ You pressed "Allow"`, `✓ You answered "…"`, `✓ You typed "allow" in the chat`. No line says who took it or what runs now.
- **An error**: what failed, then what happens next or what you can do. `Couldn't save the setting. Your old value stays.`
