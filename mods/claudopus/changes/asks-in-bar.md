- Everything that waits for you is now in the bar above the prompt as well as at the top of the panel: each ask, admin command, release and put-back change as a small amber row naming its work, what it asks, and its own buttons (Allow once, Always allow, Deny, or the ask's answers), newest first.
- A press in the bar answers just as a press in the panel does: the row turns green in place, the panel's box says the same at once, and a second press anywhere sends nothing. An answer that could not be sent says so in its row and offers the buttons again.
- More opens a row in place to the whole question, what it is about, what to copy and a box to reply in your own words; an admin command too long to show in one row offers only Deny until you open it and see it whole.
- Three rows show at most, with "and N more waiting" to open the rest; a key or password is still typed only in the panel's box, and another chat's ask is answered in its own chat, each shown in the bar with the press that takes you there.
- The line "N wait for you at the top of the panel" is gone, as the bar now shows them itself. In the terminal each waiting thing is a line with its answers numbered on from row to row, the same number twice sending it.
