- An ask's button answers only when you deliberately click it: a press that lands just as the panel's layout moved or scrolled under your pointer, or one made by a key (such as a space typed while the focus sat on a button), does not count. The card says so, gives your typing back to the reply box, and asks you to click again.
- A reply typed in an ask's box and not sent wins over its buttons: pressing a button then answers nothing until you send the reply or clear the box. Your words passed on from the chat while an answer waits win over it too.
- An answer that releases, allows or approves something (and any press on a screen not yet seen to click) is said back on the card ("You answered: Release it") and reaches the work only four seconds later, with a Take it back button meanwhile; in the terminal, 0 takes it back.
- In the terminal, the same answer digit pressed twice in a quick burst (as in a message starting "11") no longer answers.
