- A task that waits for hours on its own command, a watch, or something that never comes is no longer just flagged. If nothing has started it again, Claudopus starts it to check on what it waits for: first once the command's own expected time has passed (at least half an hour), then after an hour, two hours, and every four hours. A stuck task stops what's stuck or sends what it has. Its card says "Claudopus asked Claude to check on what it waits for", and the chat knows.
- A question or a press waiting on a card is no longer forgotten when you don't see the card. Claudopus notifies you again 15 minutes, an hour, four hours and twelve hours after it was asked. When you write in the chat, its reply ends with one line naming the card that waits for you, at most once an hour.
- The chat now sees what every task's card shows (working, how many to-dos are done, what it's doing, what waits for you) each time you write. It answers how things stand from the cards, not from memory.
- The chat can look at a repo other than the folder it was opened in (`git -C <folder> log`, `status`, `diff` and the like). Before, those looks were refused and the chat had to guess.
- A task now does what you send it, if it can with what it already has, instead of handing it back as "a new task of its own". It hands back only a request that has nothing to do with its own work. It no longer hands back the parts of your message the chat already passed on to another task.
- The chat no longer promises to do something later and then forgets. It starts the task now, set to wait for what it needs.
