Claudopus

The design system

Everything Claudopus shows the public is built from cells, like its octopus. The Claudopus page uses only what is defined here, and both files carry the same tokens at the top of their styles.

Colour

  • The page is near-black or warm paper, and follows the reader's light or dark setting.
  • Purple belongs to the octopus and to the one thing to press. It is never a background.
  • Green only says finished. Coral only says trouble.
  • Screens (the terminal, the video, the panel) show the app, which is dark, so they stay dark in both settings.

Type

  • Two faces, both already on the reader's machine, so no font is downloaded.
  • Sans is Helvetica Neue, then Helvetica, then Arial: the face of the wordmark and of the video's sentences.
  • Mono is the system's own (SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas): commands, paths and small notes.
  • Headings are bold and set tight. Body text is regular. Nothing is set in capitals, and nothing is italic.
  • A page has three levels: one display line that says what the reader gets, head-size titles for the few parts they came for, and title-size headings for the reference below.
  • Every size on the page is one of the seven below. On a screen, words are small and facts are note size.
  • The words say what the reader gets, in words anyone knows, before anything else. The reader is never left to work out the problem.
  • Sentences are whole, short and plain: an add-on, a job, a panel, a result.

Space

  • One cell is 4 pixels. Every gap, padding and size is a whole number of cells.
  • The steps are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32 cells.
  • The page is 1120 wide at most, text 640. Sections sit 24 cells apart, 16 on a phone.
  • Everything hangs from one left edge.
  • The one thing to do, install, is the only part that sits whole in a raised box.

The pixel motif

  • Nothing has a round corner. Corners step in by one cell, or two on a big box.
  • Nothing has an outline, a soft shadow, a gradient or a blur. Boxes are flat fills.
  • A rule is a row of cells.
  • A list marks each line with one cell, coloured by what the line says.
  • Numerals, icons and marks are drawn on a cell grid, never typed as symbols. Numerals are three cells wide and five tall.
  • Numerals count real steps only. Sections are not numbered.

The octopus

  • It has three states, as in the mod: resting, working, and happy when work finishes.
  • It is purple. Only the happy one is green.
  • The drawings are the mod's own files, on a 16 or 24 cell grid, and are never redrawn by hand.
  • It is shown at whole multiples of its grid, so every cell stays square and sharp.
  • It is never rotated, stretched, outlined, shadowed or put on a purple ground.
  • The big octopus appears once, at the top, and may look at the pointer, one cell at a time.
  • A small one sits only where work is shown or done: in the panel, and on the install lines.

Buttons and code

  • A button is a purple face with dark words, resting one cell above its shadow. Pressed, it drops onto the shadow.
  • Purple buttons are the page's few real actions: install, play, open the code. Any other button is the quiet one.
  • Links are underlined in purple, two pixels thick.
  • Commands sit on a screen, in mono, behind a purple prompt mark, each with its own copy key.
  • The part of a command that names Claudopus is light purple. A copied key turns green for two seconds. If copying fails, the line is selected and the key says so.
  • Short code inside a sentence sits on a raised fill.

Everything it keeps is in ~/.claude/claudopus as plain files.

Motion

  • Things move the way the octopus does: in steps, on a beat of a quarter second.
  • Nothing eases, fades slowly, scrolls smoothly, slides in on scroll or bounces.
  • A press moves a button one cell. A finish is two seconds of cheering, then back to work.
  • Only what shows work is allowed to keep moving: the octopus, the three dots, a clock, and the small picture of the panel.
  • The video never starts by itself.
  • When the reader asks for less motion, everything holds its resting frame.
